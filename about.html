About NameNests

Welcome to NameNests — a place to explore beautiful baby names and discover the stories and meanings behind them.

Our Story

Choosing a name for a baby is a special journey. We created NameNests to make that journey easier, enjoyable and meaningful for parents and families.

What We Offer

NameNests helps you explore baby names for boys, girls and unisex choices. You can search names, explore different origins, save your favourite names and copy names and meanings easily.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make baby name discovery simple and accessible for families around the world. We aim to keep improving our collection and make it easier to find a name that feels right.

A Note About Name Meanings

Name meanings and origins can vary across languages, cultures and traditions. We encourage parents to explore different interpretations and verify important details before making a final choice.

Thank You

Thank you for visiting NameNests and being part of our journey. We hope you find a name that feels like home.
